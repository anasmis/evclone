Untethered or Tethered EV charger - which is right for you?

An Untethered EV charger gives you the flexibility to use your own cable, which can be more cost-effective and adaptable if you have multiple vehicles or need extra length. A Tethered charger on the other hand comes with a permanently attached cable, making it quick and convenient to plug in — ideal for busy households.

 

The best choice depends on how you park, how often you charge, and whether you prefer a built-in cable or a more flexible setup. For an in depth guide on choose a charger, take a look at our guide - Tethered vs Untethered EV Charger: Which is the Best?

FAQs about home EV charging

Installation

Non-standard charger installations may incur an additional cost, such as groundwork. Once your order is placed, we'll be in touch to better understand your requirements and produce a no-obligation quote for any additional works. If you decide not to proceed, you’ll receive a full refund.

La plupart des installations de bornes a domicile necessitent une place de stationnement dediee et accessible afin de garantir une pose securisee.

You’ll need to check that your preferred EV charger installation location is close enough to where you actually park your electric vehicle. This is because there are different electric car charging cable lengths (we recommend a compromise between ease of use and ease of storage). You may also need to consider where the charging socket is located on your EV.

Il faut aussi considerer la distance entre votre tableau electrique et l'emplacement de la borne, car les limites de cablage varient selon les projets.

Only a qualified and competent electrician who has passed the relevant qualifications can install an EV charger. They should hold a qualification for chargepoint installation (2921) and be registered with the Competent Person Scheme. You’ll find that many EV charger providers offer installation as part of the purchase. If you prefer to organise your EV charger installation yourself, you can find installateurs EV partenaires via notre equipe EVplug.

If you’re looking to use the programmes d'incitation locaux chargepoint grant scheme to reduce the cost of your EV charger installation in rented accommodation or a flat you own, you must use one of the installers authorised as part of the scheme.

You won’t need permission to install an EV charger if you live in a house you own and have private parking. If you rent your home or own a flat, you’ll have to seek permission from your landlord or property manager before arranging an installation.

While you have to seek out permission, there’s some good news. Renters and flat owners may be eligible for the programmes d'incitation locaux grant, which can lower the cost of getting an EV charger by up to £350. En savoir plus sur the grant scheme and see the full list of eligibility criteria.

Yes. Our EVplug Solo 3S chargers come with solar integration, meaning you can charge your vehicle with solar power or a mixture of solar and grid energy. Simply activate solar charging mode and set your preferences.

Solar mode - allows you to charge purely using excess solar energy - ideal for very sunny days where lot of solar energy is generated.

Solar and grid mode - allows you to use excess solar energy, topped up with a small amount of energy from the National Grid - ideal for cloudier days when you have excess solar energy you want to use up but you need some energy from the grid (EVs require a minimum of 1.4kW to begin charging).

EVplug chargers don't integrate with wider home smart tech such as smart speakers or applicances but you can control your EVplug remotely using our app. Simply download the EVplug app and connect your charger to your home Wi-Fi and you'll be able to set smart charging schedules, securely lock your charger, pick your preferred charging mode, and much more.

Yes, EVplug chargers are compatible with every energy supplier and all single and two rate energy tariffs. Our chargers are compatible with Octopus tariffs such as 'Octopus Go' but not smart tariff such as 'Intelligent octopus Go' and 'Intelligent Drive Pack'.

Si vous n'avez pas d'allee privee, vous pouvez toujours rouler en electrique grace au reseau public EVplug accessible avec la carte EVplug. There are also options available with our partners such as Kerbocharge who make owning an EV charger with on-street parking easier than ever.

If you live in a flat and need some help convincing your property manager or landlord to install electric vehicle charging in your apartment, use our letter template to kickstart the conversation.

We recommend always having your charger connected to Wi-Fi, as this allows you to make the most of the smart features included with your EVplug. These benefits include managing your charging schedule using the EVplug app, using 'Off mode' in the app to remotely lock your charger when you're not using it, and making it easy to keep your charger software up-to-date with over-the-air updates.

If your Wi-Fi does drop out though, don’t worry. Your EV will continue to charge, it just means that you will lose access to smart features for this period.
