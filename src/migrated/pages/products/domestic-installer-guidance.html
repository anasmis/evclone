Why install Pod Point home EV chargers?

Lower your cost of install

No type B RCD required. Only Type A

Award-winning EV charger

With charging speeds of up to 7.4kW.

Installation support

Tech support available should you need it.

Easier to install

Reduce DNO applications with no earth rod required.

Reduce cost, time and complexity

Our Solo 3S home EV charger has been designed with an easier and more efficient installation in mind:
 

  • Quick and simple configuration and commissioning through the intuitive Pod Point Installer app.
  • The Solo 3S charger has built-in earth-neutral failure protection - no earth rod or isolation transformer required.
  • Load balancing reduces the number of applications for pre-authorisation required from the DNO.
  • Every domestic Solo 3S is fitted with 6mA DC leakage protection - no type B RCD required.

  • Easy-to-follow, real-world installer training

Support where you need it

Whether you're an experienced installer or expanding your services into EV charging, partnering with Pod sets you up for success.

  1. Complete the free online training to offer a 5-year customer warranty
  2. Once you've completed the training, we’ll share a package of 'Approved Installer' assets you can use on your website.
  3. Download the Installer app and set up your account, ready to commission installs.
  4. If you need technical support, you can call 0207 247 4114 and select option 4, then option 2 to talk to our team.

Note: If your company has more than one electrician, each team member will need to complete the training to qualify for the enhanced 5-year warranty.
