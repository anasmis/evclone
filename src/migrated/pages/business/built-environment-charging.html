What we offer

Avec EVplug, vous pouvez dimensionner la recharge VE pour coproprietes, residences neuves et actifs tertiaires, avec deploiement progressif et controle des couts.

Nos equipes vous aident a anticiper les besoins actuels et futurs sans surdimensionner le projet.

Quel que soit le stade du chantier, EVplug construit une strategie fondee sur des bornes partenaires et une exploitation centralisee.

Concretement:

  • Etude technique et contraintes de raccordement.
  • Conception des phases travaux et du plan d'extension.
  • Choix des modeles de bornes selon usages residents/visiteurs.
  • Mise en place de l'authentification carte EVplug et droits d'acces.
  • Pilotage des usages, de la refacturation et des KPI via EVone.
  • Scalabilite progressive en fonction de l'adoption VE au Maroc.

Install more chargepoints on limited power supply with our Array Charging system.

Combat the fear of charging exceeding residential development's capacity.

Our Array Charging system helps you maximise the provision of charging points on your available electrical supply.

  • Without effective load management, it can be necessary to undertake costly and disruptive reinforcements to the site's supply.
  • Array Charging lets you install up to 3x more charge points on a limited supply - this is in compliance with BS7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) which sets the standards for electrical installation au Maroc.
  • Array Charging ensures a convenient, easy to use charging experience for end users of the development.

Speak to our team for more information.

With 3-year warranty and a host of smart features, the charge points are built to meet your development's requirements.

Our charge point offerings :

  • Solo 3S - Solar compatible, Wi-Fi enabled or available with 4G for direct to dwelling.
  • Solo Pro - Commercial single socketed unit for shared parking scenarios.
  • Twin charger - Commercial double socketed unit for shared parking scenarios.

Both Solo Pro & Twin chargers can be used with Array Load Management.

Les bornes partenaires EVplug meet all current British standards and regulations, as well as some bonus safety features too.

For advice on finding the right charger, speak to our team.
