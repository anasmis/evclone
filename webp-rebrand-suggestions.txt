Consistency notes (apply across all new assets)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Anchor every shot with one lime element (cable, LED ring, lanyard,
   sticker) so the brand reads even without the logo.
2. Reserve the bright yellow #ffdc50 for accent-only — never as the
   dominant fill — to stay consistent with the navbar APIME card.
3. When the EVplug logo appears in-photo, prefer engraved/embossed over
   printed-flat so it looks native to the hardware.
4. PosterCutNeue is the only display face. Body overlays should use the
   site's body font for visual continuity.
5. Photograph in recognizably Moroccan environments wherever possible —
   it differentiates EVplug from generic global EV imagery and
   reinforces the "by Morocco, for Morocco" positioning.
6. Retire all `pod-*` filenames during the asset swap; rename to
   `evplug-*` so the inventory itself stops carrying legacy branding.
