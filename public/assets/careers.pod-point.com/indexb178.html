We’re Pod – one of the UK’s leading EV charging providers, trusted by over 250,000 customers and powering more than 5 million miles every single day. But behind the numbers is a team of passionate people working together to shape a smarter, more sustainable future.

We started off in 2009 as Pod Point, helping people make the switch to electric as EVs began to hit the road. Today, we’re leading the way into a smarter energy future. As part of the EDF family – the UK’s biggest generator of zero-carbon electricity – we have the strength and stability to grow for the future.

We’re proud to be the trusted charging partner for leading automotive brands like Mercedes, Jaguar Land Rover, BMW, and Kia; home builders Barratt and Bellway; and retail giants including Tesco. Our smart charging solutions don’t just support EV drivers - they help balance the grid, lower costs, and make clean energy accessible to everyone.

Life at Pod

At Pod, our power is your opportunity. We believe in the strength of our people to drive change, deliver for customers, and grow together. We foster a supportive, inclusive environment where collaboration, care, and ambition go hand in hand. 

Whether you're an engineer, a designer, a customer support specialist, or a data analyst, everyone is empowered to make a difference—for our customers, our communities, and the planet.

Our Values

For us at Pod, we unite around our three guiding principles of edge, truth and care, that evolved to become our values:
‘Lead the Charge’, 'What's Right, Not Easy' and ‘Connected Heart’. 

Lead the Charge

What's Right, Not Easy

We’re committed to doing the right thing, even when it’s tough. Integrity and honesty are at the core of everything we do.

Connected Heart

We care deeply about our people, our customers, and our community. Building strong, positive connections is essential to our success.
