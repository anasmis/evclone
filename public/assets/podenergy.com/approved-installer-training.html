Pod's approved domestic installer training

Become an approved domestic installer & offer a 5-year product warranty*

Offer your customers one of the UK’s most trusted EV home chargers with zero hassle and enhanced installer support.

Book your session

Registration now open

Want to offer your customers one of the UK’s most trusted EV home chargers – and install it with zero fuss?
 

Our brand new Pod domestic installer training is designed by an installer, for installers, and it’s now open to all certified electricians.
 

It offers easy-to-follow, practical training, including a deep dive into our updated Installer app, which offers smart new features to make every install faster and easier.

In this 2-hour online course, you'll get:

✅ Easy-to-follow, real-world installer training
✅ A deep dive into our updated Installer App
✅ To learn about our App-supported fault diagnosis
✅ A live Q&A with a member of our tech team
✅ A look at the support we’ve put in place

And once you’re approved…

🎁 You’ll unlock a 5-year product warranty for completing the course, and get access to our approved installer marketing assets.


⚡No waffle, or jargon. Just solid training to help you get Pod home EV charger installations done, fast and simple.
