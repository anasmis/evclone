Is our Home Fleet solution right for you?

Suitable for: drivers with off-street parking, where vehicles are based at home overnight, and there’s regular overnight dwell time.

Not suitable for: depot-based vehicles, or where rapid mid-shift charging is required at a workplace. It's also not suited to drivers without their own driveway or ability to connect a charger to their own energy supply.
