To be eligible for the EVplug Energie tariff you must:

  • Own a EVplug charger or purchase one through the Pod online shop
  • Own an electric vehicle 
  • Have a smart meter installed or be willing to have one fitted.
  • Pay for your tariff by direct debit

Peak rates and standing charges vary depending on your home address. To see your personalised rates, you'll need a custom tariff quote from partenaire energie. To get started, click here.
