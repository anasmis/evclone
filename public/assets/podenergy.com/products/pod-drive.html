Pod Drive explained

Our friends at WhatCar? break down what Pod Drive really means.

Your charger

We fit the latest Pod Point Solo 3S premium charger. Over 250,000 Pod Points are already installed in the UK.

7.4kWh is the fastest charger that can be fitted to the typical home (you need industrial 3-phase electricity to fit anything faster) so you’ll get the fastest charging possible.

We fit our premium tethered model (which has a built-in 5m cable) for maximum convenience. So, you can plug straight in. Keep your charging cables in your car for when you’re out and about. 

Your charger is controlled over Wi-Fi. It has to be connected to your own full-time Wi-Fi network to function properly.

If you’ve got, or plan to get, solar panels, the Solo 3S is fully solar ready to help you charge your EV for free using your solar power.

Our team of Which? endorsed installers will fit the charger to your home. It takes a couple of hours and they take care of everything.

How Miles work

It's simple: When you smart charge, we reward you with Miles – our way of paying you back for being flexible. These Miles are converted into cash which you can withdraw into your bank account - effectively reducing your driving costs.

We’ll add charge to your EV when low-cost energy is available.

Your energy company supplies the electricity used to smart charge your EV, so you pay your bill as usual. We support all energy providers and most energy tariffs on the market.

We pay you 2.3p per mile (calculated by assuming an average efficiency of an EV at 3.5 miles per kWh and a typical overnight charging cost of 8.05p per kWh). That equals £172.50 cash paid to you - equivalent to 7,500 driving miles.

Your app

Welcome to the Pod Home app. The command centre for your charger. It puts everything you need to manage, track, and optimise your charging at your fingertips.

The Pod Home app tells the charger when to charge to make the most of low-cost energy and keeps you up to date with everything. Just set your charge target and tell us when you want your EV ready by – for example, 80% charge by 7am – and the app does the rest.

See how many Miles you’ve earned and how many you have left to earn.

You’ll earn Miles after every smart charge (up to your annual Miles allowance), which you can withdraw as cash. Once you’ve earned £10 worth of Miles, you can withdraw the cash balance directly into your bank account. Either withdraw as you go or save it up – the choice is yours! 

Connect and switch easily between multiple EVs and if you have a guest, you can charge their EV too.

If you ever need instant power, use the ‘Boost’ function to override smart charging. Any charging using ‘Boost’ won’t earn rewards.

View comprehensive breakdowns of your charging activity – including charging duration, costs, and energy usage - all at the touch of a button.

Your warranty

A warranty that never runs out of road. Our pioneering Repair/Replace/Recharge warranty ensures that you always have a charged-up EV, supporting you as long as your subscribed to Pod Drive.

Your 3-year agreement

Here’s the deal. You’ll get a premium charging system, next-level support and included miles. You agree to use our service for three years so we can keep the lights on at Pod HQ.

All-inclusive means all-inclusive: your agreement covers everything, with no extra hidden charges.

After three years the home charger will remain yours. We’ll contact you before your initial Pod Drive agreement comes to an end to discuss extending your subscription at a reduced cost, so you can continue to use your Pod Point and benefit from inclusive miles, stress-free warranty, and lifetime cover.

Pod Drive is a 3-year service agreement your inclusive miles allowance, lifetime warranty, and 48-hour repair/replace/recharge guarantee all form part of the service you’ll get. We’re a responsible company so we make sure you can afford Pod Drive before we let you sign.

If you move home, we offer a range of options to ensure you can continue your Pod Drive subscription seamlessly, just call our support team when you know your moving date.
