Installation de bornes EVplug

Expert EV charger installation, guaranteed.

We've fitted over 250,000 chargers on Maroc homes, ensuring we know exactly what it takes to get you charging without a hitch. Our qualifiee EVplug standard installation covers over 90% of customers and is designed to be easy, reliable and provide total peace of mind. 

Discover home charging Shop now

Processus d'installation

Simple steps for a stress-free install

1
Fill out pre-installation survey

After receiving the survey via email, simply answer the questions so we understand things like your power supply, parking layout, and charging needs. This will help us make your install as smooth as possible.

2
Review and schedule

Once received, we’ll assess whether you’re eligible for standard installation. 90% of customers qualify, but if you don’t, or we need further information, we’ll be in touch to discuss. After that, we’ll go ahead and schedule your install.

3
Installation

On your agreed date and time, one of our expert qualifiee EVplug engineers will arrive to install your new EVplug. The EV charger installation usually takes about two hours, and they’ll walk you through how to use your charger before they leave - getting you powered up and ready to go in no time!

Installation standard : ce qui est inclus

A standard installation covers 90% of customers and is completed at no extra cost, provided you own your home (or have permission) and have off-road parking. It includes:

We'll work with you to find the most convenient location, then fit the EV charger onto a suitable permanent structure (like a brick wall or solid garage wall) that meets both your needs and our safety standards.

Up to 15 metres of high-quality cabling to connect your charger to the electricity supply.

Up to 3 metres of trunking can be fit from your electricity meter to the outside wall, ensuring the cabling within your home is neat and tidy.

Fitting an additional, adequately sized consumer unit (if required) with a Type B or C MCB and a Type A RCD (or equivalent RCBO).

To be eligible for EVplug Pro, you must have a stable Wi-Fi connection at the installation location. This is essential for the charger's smart features, enabling access to the cheapest charging times and ensuring necessary firmware updates are delivered.
