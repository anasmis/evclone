Let's break it down...

Get signed up

Download the brand-new Pod Home app to sign up to Pod Rewards. A cost-free subscription.


 

Get smart charging

Just tell us when you need your vehicle ready by, and we’ll create a custom charging schedule, based on when energy is cheapest for you — automatically using off-peak hours if you're on a two-rate tariff.

Get earning

After every smart charging session, you’ll earn rewards in your Wallet. Once you've earned £10 worth of rewards, you'll be able to withdraw as cash into your bank account. Nice!

Get boosted

If your plans change and you need to charge straight away, you can ‘Boost’ to override your smart charging schedule.

The finer details.
All charging costs and cash reward amounts assume average EV driving efficiency of 3.5 miles per kWh. Customers can earn up to a total of £103.50 per year in cash rewards, through our reward miles, from the day of sign up. A ‘mile’ is not equivalent to a real-world diving mile. Each time a smart charging session is completed, customers will earn rewards. Pod Rewards miles are earned at a rate of 0.875 miles per kWh charged and paid at 2.3p per mile. Maximum £103.50 rewards per customer, per year, based on individual charging behaviour. You will still need to pay your energy supplier for your electricity as normal. Read the full terms and conditions here.
